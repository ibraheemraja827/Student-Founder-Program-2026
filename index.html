SFP 2026 is the next edition of the Student Founder Program, building on the vision of SFP 2025. It brings together emerging leaders, entrepreneurs, companies, and students to connect, learn, and collaborate. The program focuses on leadership, innovation, technology, and entrepreneurship, creating opportunities for meaningful ideas and future partnerships.

We aim to connect young talent with companies, founders, and innovators to build a stronger entrepreneurial future.
